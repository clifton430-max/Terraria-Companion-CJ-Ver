# Terraria Companion

A desktop companion app (CJ Ver): a team of Terraria-style professionals in suits who live on the macOS desktop, plus a Terraria-style world drawn behind everything that they (or you) can play in.

## Language

### The world

**Background world**:
The Terraria-style world drawn as a desktop background layer, under Finder's desktop icons and widgets and behind every app window. It keeps running whether or not other windows are open or focused.
_Avoid_: Ambient world, world overlay, wallpaper

**Pan chord**:
The user-customisable combination of modifier keys (default ⌥⌘) held while dragging or scrolling on the bare desktop to move the view across the background world.
_Avoid_: Drag shortcut, pan key

**Explore**:
Looking around and editing the background world directly with mouse and keys.

**Play Terraria**:
The mode where the user controls a companion in the background world.

**Let the team play**:
The mode where the companions play through the background world on their own.
_Avoid_: Auto-play, adventure mode

### Maps

**Map**:
A desktop theme: falling or drifting particles, a decoration under the menu bar and a decoration along the Dock. Current maps are Cherry Blossom, Snow and the Chess map.
_Avoid_: Theme (as a separate concept), skin

**Chess map**:
The dark-fantasy map: drifting dust instead of falling particles, with fallen chess pieces along the Dock. Not related to the playable chessboard.
_Avoid_: Chess board theme, Hollow Knight map

### Companions

**Main companion**:
The single selected companion; the one voice input addresses.
_Avoid_: Primary companion, active companion

**Built-in name**:
A character's own name from the roster; she uses it for herself in bubbles, chat and AI answers.

**Notch resident**:
The one companion living inside CustomNotch, dropped or thrown into it from the desktop. She keeps her look, gestures and chatter there; what she says waits in the notch (which glows pink) until it's opened, and she comes back out when dragged out or when CustomNotch quits.
_Avoid_: Notch pet, notch companion (as a separate character)

**Pouch**:
The compact inventory opened from the notch's inventory button: the same arsenal as the menu's Combat section, in a small dark panel beside the notch that matches CustomNotch's look.
_Avoid_: Mini menu, notch inventory window

**Custom name**:
A name the user gives a companion, shown in the roster, settings and the Terraria HUD and journal in place of the built-in name.
_Avoid_: Nickname (nicknames are affection terms she uses for the user)

### Gathering

**Good tree**:
A tree a companion can stand beside at its trunk base and chop without digging through anything built or any world structure, and not on a sky island. Trees more than 200 tiles further away than the nearest good tree are passed over until nothing closer is left.
_Avoid_: Valid tree, reachable tree

**Tree mark**:
The team's shared memory that chopping a particular tree failed; it keeps every companion away from that tree for a wait that grows with each failure (1 min, 5 min, then until dawn) and is saved with the adventure.
_Avoid_: Blacklist, blocked tree

### Voice

**Wispr Flow**:
The third-party dictation app that carries companion voice input by default.
_Avoid_: Wishr Flow, Whisper Flow

**Apple Speech push-to-talk**:
The companion's own record-and-transcribe voice input, kept as an alternative the user can switch to instead of Wispr Flow.

**Wake name**:
The main companion's built-in or custom name, optionally after a lead-in ("Hey …"), at the start of a dictation; it claims that dictation for her even when it was typed into another app.
